> whoami

My name is Cristea-Olaru Stefan, also known as solaru or SOC. I am a Security Researcher at Bitdefender with a focus on practical security research and malware analysis. In my current role, I analyze malware samples across multiple formats, including binaries (PE/ELF) as well as scripts such as Python, JavaScript, and VBScript. My work involves triaging and classifying threats, as well as creating signatures when necessary. I also perform initial forensic analysis and have contributed to developing generic detection mechanisms. My main interests include reverse engineering, malware analysis, binary exploitation, and building proof-of-concept implementations to better understand real-world attack techniques. I enjoy approaching security from both offensive and defensive perspectives — exploring how vulnerabilities are exploited, while also understanding how they are detected, investigated, and mitigated in real environments. I am continuously working on deepening my understanding of low-level systems, particularly PE and ELF binaries, while maintaining a broad perspective across different scripting environments used in modern attacks.
